3. Zero Personal Data Collection

Your privacy is strictly protected:

  • No Personal Information: We do not collect names, phone numbers, email addresses, or government identification.

4. Device Permissions

  • android.permission.INTERNET: Enables playback of network streams entered by the user.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Detects connectivity to notify users if an internet connection drops.
  • android.permission.READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used solely to index and play videos stored on the device or connected USB drives.
